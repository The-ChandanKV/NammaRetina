Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Contact: thechandankv@gmail.com

NammaRetina (“we”, “our”, or “the Service”) is an AI-assisted diabetic retinopathy screening research application. This policy explains what information we collect and how it is used when you visit or sign in to https://nammaretina.onrender.com/.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and authentication identifiers when you register or sign in with Google.
  • Clinical workflow data: patient demographics and retinal images that clinicians upload, plus model outputs, heatmaps, and reports generated by the Service.
  • Technical logs: basic server logs such as timestamps and request metadata needed to operate and secure the Service.

2. How we use information

  • To authenticate clinicians and operate the screening workflow.
  • To generate AI-assisted severity assessments, explanations, and PDF reports.
  • To maintain security, troubleshoot failures, and improve reliability.

We do not sell personal data. The Service is intended for authorized clinical users, not public consumer profiles.

3. Google Sign-In

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google provides us with basic profile information (such as name and email) according to the scopes you approve. We use that information only to create or identify your clinician account. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Storage and processors

Application data may be stored using cloud infrastructure such as Supabase (database, auth, and file storage) and hosting providers such as Render. Access is restricted to authenticated users of the Service and administrative operators.

5. Retention

Account and clinical records are retained while the Service is actively used for care workflows or research demonstration, unless an authorized administrator deletes them earlier. Contact us to request deletion of your account data.

6. Sharing

We do not share personal or clinical data with third parties for advertising. Data may be disclosed if required by law, or to infrastructure providers solely to host and run the Service.

7. Security

We use authentication, access controls, and encrypted transport (HTTPS) to protect data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

8. Medical disclaimer

NammaRetina provides AI-assisted decision support for research and screening workflows. It is not a standalone medical device approval, and outputs must be reviewed by a qualified clinician. It is not a substitute for professional medical judgement.
